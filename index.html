Process

From data to decision in seconds

Four independent agents run in parallel, combine into a consensus with learned weights, then pass through a multi-layer risk gate before a human approves execution.

01 · DATA

Multi-source ingestion

Price data, SEC filings, short interest, analyst ratings, VIX, and macro indicators — fetched in parallel in under 10 seconds.

02 · AGENTS

4-way agent consensus

Technical, Fundamentals, Sentiment, and Macro agents score independently. Sharpe-optimised learned weights combine their conviction into one decision.

03 · RISK

Multi-layer risk gate

VIX veto, borrow availability, squeeze risk score, earnings blackout, Kelly fraction sizing, correlation-aware capital allocation. Zero exceptions.

04 · EXECUTE

Human-approved execution

Signals arrive via Slack with full reasoning attached. One tap approves. Bracket orders (entry + stop-loss + take-profit) are sent instantly to IBKR.

Capabilities

Built for real markets

Not a backtesting toy. MATHS executes live trades with institutional-grade safeguards on every order.

Adaptive position sizing

Kelly-fraction sizing adjusted for VIX regime, portfolio heat, pair correlation, and realised transaction costs. Capital is never over-committed.

Full audit trail

Every scan decision — each agent's individual score and reasoning — persists to Firestore. Complete traceability from signal to fill, forever.

Self-learning ensemble

Weekly Sharpe-optimised weight refit from closed trades. The system learns which agents outperform in each market regime and adjusts accordingly.

Hard risk veto gate

VIX > 35 kills all new positions. Earnings blackout ±5 days. Squeeze risk scorer. Borrow availability check. No model can override these rules.

Portfolio construction

Ledoit-Wolf covariance, HRP clustering, cross-sleeve pod allocator with Sharpe-weighted risk budgets. Correlation-aware capital deployment.

Triple-barrier exits

ML model predicts TP/SL/timeout probabilities per open position. Partial profits taken dynamically. ATR trailing stops tighten as R-multiple grows.
