Business model

Digital-native by design

Briefcaster monetizes as a freemium consumer subscription — free to start, with a premium tier unlocking more sources, longer briefs, and additional voices.

MATHS will be offered as subscription access to the platform once it graduates from private beta — systematic, auditable trading intelligence as a service.

Both products are fully digital services we own and operate on Google Cloud — serverless compute, managed databases, and AI services — with no physical footprint beyond the people building them.
