About Data Concept Studio

Data Concept Studio is a software company registered in Poland in 2025 and based in Kraków. Its product is Briefcaster, an iPhone app that turns the news topics, RSS feeds and YouTube channels a listener follows into a short personal audio briefing.

Legally, it is the sole proprietorship of its founder, Michał Szarek, trading under the name Data Concept Studio. It builds, owns and operates Briefcaster itself, on Google Cloud, and its revenue will come from the people who listen.

Why the company exists

Newsrooms and large media companies have editors who read everything and tell their audience what matters. Individual readers have the opposite: more sources than time, and apps that decide for them what to show next.

Language models and natural speech synthesis make a different arrangement possible: a briefing written for one person, from the sources that person chose, in that person's language. Briefcaster is our attempt to build it well, honestly labelled as AI-generated, and priced so that the people who listen are the ones paying for it.

How Briefcaster is built

A one-person company can run a consumer app reliably only by leaning on managed services and automating the work a team would otherwise do. Briefcaster runs entirely on Google Cloud.

AI

Gemini on Vertex AI writes every briefing script from the material collected for that listener. Google Cloud Text-to-Speech narrates it, with a second speech provider for some voices.

Service and data

Serverless containers run the API and the briefing pipeline, a managed PostgreSQL database holds accounts and feeds, and object storage keeps the generated audio. Infrastructure is defined as code and changed through review.

Operations

Automated tests gate every change, and the service watches itself: health checks and alerts that reach a human when briefings stop being produced.

Briefings are generated by AI end to end, and the app says so. We treat that disclosure as part of the product; our terms describe how it meets Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
