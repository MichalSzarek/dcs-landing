Product updates

What we've shipped

A running log of the work coming out of Data Concept Studio. We are a small company — this page is the honest record of what actually reached users, newest first.

September 2026 Briefcaster

Daily technology and business briefings, in Polish and English

We added a new set of ready-made daily briefings covering technology and business, hosted by two new voice pairs — one Polish, one English. They sit alongside the existing morning and science briefings, so a new listener has something to play the moment they open the app, before building a personal feed.

September 2026 Briefcaster In progress

Android version in development

Briefcaster is being brought to Android. The backend, deep links, and account-management flows required by Google Play are already in place, and the store listing is being prepared. We are not announcing a release date until the app is through review.

August 2026 Briefcaster

Shared briefings now play a preview in the browser

When someone shares a briefing, the link now opens a branded page that plays a short audio sample straight away — no install required to hear what Briefcaster sounds like.

August 2026 Briefcaster

Briefings start playing while they are still being made

Generating a briefing on demand used to mean waiting for the whole episode. Audio now streams as it is produced, so the first words play far sooner. We also expanded the voice catalogue and added saved sources, so a feed can be rebuilt from material you have already picked.

July 2026 Milestone

Briefcaster is live on the Apple App Store

Our first product passed Apple review and shipped to the App Store. Briefcaster turns the topics, RSS feeds, and YouTube channels you choose into short personalized audio briefings, written by AI and narrated with natural voices. Briefings are clearly marked as AI-generated inside the app.

2026 MATHS

MATHS runs continuously in private beta

Our second product operates in production as a private beta. Four specialist AI agents — technical, fundamentals, sentiment, and macro — reach a consensus that has to clear a hard risk gate before anything is acted on. Through 2026 we have concentrated on the parts that are unglamorous and decisive: reconciliation between our records and the broker's, auditable decision trails, and automated checks that catch a stalled pipeline.

2025 Company

Data Concept Studio is founded in Kraków

The company is registered in Poland as a product business: software we build, own, and operate ourselves, rather than client work. Everything we run is hosted on Google Cloud.
