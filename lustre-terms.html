3. What Lustre is

Lustre turns your screen into an adjustable light for your face and shows a mirrored preview from the front camera, with a warmth and brightness dial, built-in presets (Daylight, Evening, Office, Golden Hour and Max), custom presets, layouts (mirror on top, mirror below, full screen, and on foldable phones held half-open the mirror on one side of the fold and the controls on the other), a symmetry grid, zoom, pause and photo capture.

  • Not a medical or cosmetic device. Lustre is a screen showing light and a camera preview. It does not diagnose, treat or assess anything.
  • Suggestions, not measurements. Preset warmth describes how the light looks. The room preset suggestion (iPhone only) is based on the camera's own white-balance estimate, and the face-lighting hint only indicates which side of your face, if any, appears darker. Both are suggestions, not measurements.
