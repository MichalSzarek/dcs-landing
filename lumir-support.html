Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Lumir has no account, no sign-in and no password. The free baseline — mirror, screen light, all four built-in presets, and photo capture — works the moment you open the app.

What do the preset names and Kelvin values mean?

Daylight (5500 K), Evening (3000 K), Office (4200 K) and Golden Hour (3800 K) describe how the light looks, not a calibrated measurement. Lumir is not a photometric instrument, and the same preset is not guaranteed to look identical on two different iPhones or under different ambient light.

What does Lumir Pro unlock?

Exactly one thing: creating and editing your own custom presets. Everything else — the mirror, the screen light, brightness and warmth control, all four built-in presets, split/full layouts, and photo capture — is free and stays free. Lumir Pro is a one-time purchase: no subscription, no recurring charge, nothing to cancel.

How do I restore Lumir Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Use Restore Purchases in the app. It asks Apple what your Apple Account owns and restores Pro at no extra charge — there is no licence key or code to keep.

If I get a refund, do I lose the presets I already made?

No. Your existing custom presets stay on your device, and you can still see, apply and delete them. Only creating a new one or editing an existing one requires Pro again. Deleting a preset is never restricted, with or without Pro.

Does Lumir work without camera access?

Yes — that's a designed mode, not a degraded one. Without camera access, Lumir is still fully usable as a screen light with all four presets. You can grant or withdraw camera access any time in Settings → Privacy & Security or Settings → Lumir.

Can I use Lumir as a webcam for video calls?

No. Lumir does not integrate with any calling or video-conferencing app and cannot supply video to one. Using your phone as a light source next to a laptop is a use of the light, not a webcam integration.

Where do my photos go?

Photos you capture with the shutter are saved to your iPhone's photo library, mirrored to match what you saw on screen. Lumir only ever adds photos — it cannot read, browse or delete your existing library. What happens next (iCloud Photos, Shared Albums, and so on) is controlled by your own iOS settings.

How do I delete my presets or all my Lumir data?

Delete a single preset from within the app at any time. To remove everything, delete the Lumir app itself — iOS removes its entire on-device data folder with it. There is no server-side copy to ask anyone to delete, because Lumir does not transmit your presets or settings anywhere.
