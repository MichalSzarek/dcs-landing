How it works

From your sources to your ears

No scrolling, no tab overload. Pick what you care about and Briefcaster does the reading for you.

01 · SOURCES

Pick your sources

Choose news topics, add RSS feeds, follow YouTube channels. Your brief covers exactly what you care about — nothing else.

02 · AI

AI curates and scripts

Briefcaster reads everything new across your sources, picks what matters, and writes a tight, conversational script — just for you.

Listen on your schedule

Features

Built for how you actually listen

Truly personal

Every brief is generated for one listener: you. Your topics, your feeds, your channels — no editorial one-size-fits-all.

RSS + YouTube ingestion

Beyond headlines: follow niche blogs via RSS and get the substance of YouTube videos without watching them.

Natural voices

State-of-the-art text-to-speech that sounds like a host, not a robot — in English and Polish.

Short by design

Briefs are minutes, not hours. Get up to speed in the time it takes to make coffee — with sources linked if you want to go deeper.

On your schedule

A fresh brief every morning, or on demand whenever you ask for one. Your feed keeps every episode ready to replay.

Share what's worth hearing

Every episode gets a link you can send to anyone — they can listen in the browser, no app required.
