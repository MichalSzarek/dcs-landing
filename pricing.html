Pricing & business model

How Briefcaster is priced, and our planned business model

Briefcaster is free to download and use today. Our planned revenue stream is a paid consumer subscription, which is not yet available and has no price set. We do not sell client work or consulting. This page separates what is available today from what we plan to offer.

How the company is funded

Today

Bootstrapped and founder-funded. We have taken no institutional investment, no venture capital, and no outside equity.

Cost base

Fully digital. Our largest running cost is cloud infrastructure and AI inference on Google Cloud — there is no physical product and no inventory.

Path to revenue

A Briefcaster subscription, sold through the App Store and, once the Android version ships, Google Play. Recurring revenue from software we own, not billable hours.

We keep this page honest rather than aspirational: if something is free, it says free; if something is planned, it says planned. When Briefcaster premium launches, its tiers and prices will be published here and in the App Store listing before any charge is made. We may also offer an optional way to unlock extra briefings by watching an ad; it would be opt-in, and the privacy policy already describes how it works.
