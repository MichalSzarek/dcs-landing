Pricing & business model

How our products are priced —
and how we make money

Data Concept Studio is a product company: we earn from the software we build and operate ourselves, sold directly to the people who use it. No client work, no consulting, no agency retainers. This page states exactly where that stands today — including the parts that are still free.

Briefcaster

Consumer iOS app · live on the App Store
Available now
Free

Briefcaster is free to download and free to use. There is no paywall in the app today — you can create your feeds and listen to your briefings without paying anything.

  • ✓ Personalized audio briefings from your own topics, RSS feeds, and YouTube channels
  • ✓ AI-written scripts, narrated with natural voices
  • ✓ Multiple languages, including English and Polish
  • ✓ A monthly usage allowance that covers everyday listening
Download on the App Store
Planned
Premium

Our planned revenue stream for Briefcaster is a consumer subscription. It is not live yet and no prices have been set — we are growing the free product first and will publish the tiers here before anything is charged.

MATHS

Multi-agent AI trading platform · private beta

MATHS is our second product and is currently in private beta, running in production for internal use. It is not sold today. When it opens beyond the beta, access will be offered as a subscription to the platform — systematic, auditable trading intelligence delivered as a service rather than as bespoke software.

MATHS is a research and decision-support platform. It is not investment advice, and we do not manage money for third parties.

How the company is funded

Today

Bootstrapped and founder-funded. We have taken no institutional investment, no venture capital, and no outside equity.

Cost base

Fully digital. Our largest running cost is cloud infrastructure and AI inference on Google Cloud — there is no physical product and no inventory.

Path to revenue

Consumer subscriptions for Briefcaster, platform subscriptions for MATHS. Recurring digital revenue from software we own — not billable hours.

We keep this page honest rather than aspirational: if something is free, it says free; if something is planned, it says planned. When Briefcaster premium launches, its tiers and prices will be published here and in the App Store listing before any charge is made.
