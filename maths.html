Process

From data to decision in seconds

Four independent agents run in parallel, combine into a consensus with learned weights, then pass through a multi-layer risk gate before a human approves execution.

01 · DATA

Multi-source ingestion

Price data, SEC filings, short interest, analyst ratings, VIX, and macro indicators — fetched in parallel in under 10 seconds.

02 · AGENTS

4-way agent consensus

Technical, Fundamentals, Sentiment, and Macro agents score independently. Sharpe-optimised learned weights combine their conviction into one decision.

03 · RISK

Multi-layer risk gate

Every signal clears a series of independent safety checks before any capital is committed. Hard rules activate automatically under adverse conditions — no model can override them.

04 · EXECUTE

Human-approved execution

Signals arrive on your phone with full reasoning attached. One tap approves. Bracket orders — entry, stop-loss, and take-profit — are routed to your broker instantly.

Capabilities

Built for real markets

Not a backtesting toy. MATHS executes live trades with institutional-grade safeguards on every order.

Adaptive position sizing

Position sizes adapt dynamically to market conditions, portfolio concentration, and signal quality. Capital is always sized relative to actual edge — never over-committed.

Full audit trail

Every scan decision — each agent's individual score and reasoning — is permanently logged. Complete traceability from signal to fill, always available.

Self-learning ensemble

The system continuously learns from closed trades, automatically reweighting agents based on what actually works in current market conditions.

Hard risk veto gate

Hard-coded rules halt new positions automatically when market conditions turn adverse — volatility spikes, upcoming events, or unfavourable liquidity. No signal can override them.

Portfolio construction

Positions are sized relative to each other, not just individually. Correlated trades share a risk budget — capital flows where the edge is highest, with cross-strategy concentration limits enforced automatically.

Triple-barrier exits

Exit decisions adapt as trades develop. Partial profits can be taken early when elevated risk is detected. Stops tighten automatically as positions move in your favour.

Infrastructure

Cloud-native from day one

Built entirely on Google Cloud with fully IaC-managed infrastructure via Terraform. Every component is observable, reproducible, and independently scalable.

Serverless compute & event-driven orchestration
Scales to zero between scans, to full capacity in milliseconds. Every pipeline is fault-tolerant with automatic retries.
State-of-the-art AI · document retrieval
Leading language models synthesise qualitative signals. Retrieval-augmented generation grounds conclusions in real financial documents.
Multi-tier persistence
Real-time event store, relational analytics, and a model-training warehouse — each purpose-built for its workload.
Full-stack observability
Distributed tracing across every service. Latency, error rates, and scan metrics monitored continuously — issues surface before they affect trades.
Security & reproducibility
Secrets managed centrally, infrastructure defined as code, build artefacts immutable and versioned. Auditable from day one.
