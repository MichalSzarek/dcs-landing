Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iPhone app Lustre: Mirror and Light (the "App") handles information. Lustre is published by Data Concept Studio Michał Szarek ("we", "us", "our"). The App is a mirror (a mirrored front-camera preview) and uses your screen as an adjustable light.

In short: Lustre has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The App's own code makes no network requests. Your camera image is processed on your device only and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. We receive no personal data from the App.

1. Who we are

The publisher of Lustre, and the data controller for any email you send us, is Data Concept Studio Michał Szarek, a sole proprietorship (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza) registered in Poland and based in Kraków, Poland. For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@dataconceptstudio.com.

2. What Lustre collects

Nothing is sent to us. Lustre has no account and no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. The App's own code makes no network requests. The only communication with a server is Apple's StoreKit talking to Apple for the trial check, the price, a purchase and restoring a purchase (see section 5).

3. Camera, photos and on-device features

3.1 Camera

Lustre asks for camera access to show a mirrored preview from the front camera. The permission text iOS shows you is: "Lustre shows a mirrored preview from the front camera. Your camera image stays on your device and is never uploaded."

The live preview is processed on your device only. It is never recorded, stored or uploaded. You can deny camera access and still use Lustre as a light.

3.2 Photos

Lustre asks only for permission to add photos to your library. A photo is saved to your Photos library only when you take one. The App never reads your photo library. Once saved, a photo is an ordinary item in your library, and your own iOS and iCloud Photos settings apply to it.

3.3 Room preset suggestion

To suggest a preset that matches the light in your room, Lustre reads the camera's own white-balance estimate (a colour temperature) in memory. This is an estimate, not a measurement. Nothing about it is stored or sent.

3.4 Face-lighting hint (off by default)

The face-lighting hint is off by default. You can turn it on in Settings ("Uneven light on your face"). When it is on, Lustre uses Apple's on-device Vision face-rectangle detection and compares the brightness of the two cheek areas. The only result is which side, if any, is darker.

No face data, image, measurement or biometric identifier is stored or transmitted. This feature is not face recognition: it does not identify who you are.

4. What Lustre stores on your device

Lustre keeps a few small files inside its own app container on your iPhone. None of them contain personal data, and none of them are sent anywhere by the App.

FileWhat it is for
settings.jsonYour app settings.
presets.jsonCustom presets you create.
trial-clock.jsonThe date the App first ran. Used only in Apple test environments such as TestFlight and the sandbox.
trial-start.jsonThe trial start date Apple last confirmed, so the trial is not reopened when Apple cannot be reached.

Deleting the App deletes these files. Standard iOS backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include them, under your own backup settings.

5. Apple, the free trial and purchases

Lustre uses Apple's StoreKit to check your free trial, show the price, make a purchase and restore a purchase. StoreKit communicates with Apple for these purposes. Purchases and payment are handled by Apple, and we do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's own privacy policy and terms.

6. Third parties, analytics, advertising and tracking

  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. Lustre does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No advertising.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because the App does not collect any.

7. When you email us

If you write to contact@dataconceptstudio.com, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. The "Contact us" row in the App's Settings opens your own mail app with a draft that already contains the App version, your iOS version and your device model, to help us answer. The App sends nothing itself: the draft stays in your mail app until you send it, and you can edit or delete any part of it first.

We use your email only to answer you, and keep the correspondence only as long as needed for that. Data Concept Studio Michał Szarek is the controller of this data.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Custom presets can be deleted in the App.
  • Everything the App stores is deleted when you delete the App.
  • Photos you took stay in your Photos library, and you delete them there like any other photo.
  • Camera and Photos access can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings → Lustre, or Settings → Privacy & Security.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because the App sends us no personal data, these rights in practice concern any email correspondence with us. To exercise them, write to contact@dataconceptstudio.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

10. Children

Lustre has no account and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of Lustre changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
