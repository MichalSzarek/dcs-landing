Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Data Concept Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Briefcaster mobile application (the "App"). Briefcaster turns news topics, RSS feeds, and YouTube channels into short, personalized audio briefs.

In short: we collect the account details you provide and the feeds you create so we can generate your briefs. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your feeds and your account at any time from within the App. Popular briefs may be shown anonymously to other listeners in the "Popular" section — never with your name or account — and you can switch this off at any time (see section 5).

1. Who we are

The data controller is Data Concept Studio. For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@dataconceptstudio.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Email address and display name — when you create an account with email and password, or sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Password — if you register with email, stored only as a salted hash; we never store your plaintext password.
  • Sign in with Apple — if you choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple's private relay address and never your real email.

2.2 Content you create

  • Feeds and sources — the topics, RSS URLs, and YouTube channels you add, plus your schedule, language, and voice preferences.
  • Generated briefs — the audio episodes we produce for you and your listening progress (so playback can resume).
  • Sharing preference — whether you allow your briefs to appear anonymously in "Popular" (for your account and for each brief), and when you saw the in-app notice about this feature (see section 5).

2.3 Optional connections

  • YouTube (read-only) — only if you tap "Connect YouTube", we request the youtube.readonly scope to list your subscriptions so you can turn them into a feed. We do not post, modify, or delete anything on your YouTube account, and you can disconnect at any time.

2.4 Device & usage information

  • Push notification token — if you enable notifications, so we can tell you when a brief is ready.
  • Usage counters — how many briefs you have generated in the current period, to apply plan limits.

2.5 Advertising identifiers (only if ads are enabled)

Briefcaster may offer an optional, ad-supported way to unlock briefs. When advertising is enabled and you consent through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and limited interaction data to serve and measure ads. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalized ads or none at all, and the App remains fully usable. Advertising is off by default.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and authenticate you.
  • To generate personalized audio briefs from your chosen sources.
  • To deliver audio playback, including in the background and on the lock screen.
  • To send you notifications and account emails (for example, password reset).
  • To enforce plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • To pick and show a daily selection of popular briefs, anonymously, to other signed-in listeners (see section 5).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

4. How your briefs are generated

To produce a brief we send the text of your selected sources (news articles, RSS items, or YouTube metadata) to large-language-model and text-to-speech providers acting on our behalf — currently Google Cloud (Vertex AI / Gemini and Cloud Text-to-Speech) and ElevenLabs. This content is processed to write a script and synthesize audio, entirely automatically and without human review of individual briefs before delivery. We do not send your account credentials to these providers. See our Terms of Service for how we disclose AI-generated content under the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).

5. Popular: anonymous sharing of briefs

The Home screen of the App includes a Popular section. Every day we pick a small selection (currently around eight) of briefs created by Briefcaster users, ranked by how many different listeners played them and how often they were listened to the end. The selection may also include briefs chosen and reviewed by our editors; these follow the same anonymity rules and sharing settings described below, and are never taken from paid or private sources. Signed-in users can play these briefs and read their transcript.

5.1 What other listeners see — and what they never see

Popular briefs are shown anonymously. Other users see only the episode itself: a title derived from the episode's content (not the name of your feed), the audio, the transcript, the show notes and article links (with query strings removed, and links to private or intranet addresses left out), the language, the duration, and the date. The audio is served from a separate anonymous copy whose address does not reveal any identifiers.

Other users never see your name, email address or account, the name or settings of your feed or brief, your list of sources or interests, or how many people listened to your brief. When you play someone else's brief, its author does not see that you listened.

5.2 Which briefs are never shared

  • Briefs built from paid or private subscription sources.
  • Briefs whose audio or transcript mentions the brief's own name.
  • Briefs that have not passed our automated content moderation.
  • Briefs removed by a moderator, and briefs reported by a listener — a report removes the brief from Popular.
  • Briefs for which you, or your account settings, have switched sharing off.

5.3 Data we use for this, and why

To rank briefs we use the playback records we already keep for every account (which episodes were played and whether they were finished). When you play a Popular brief, that playback is recorded against your own account, as for any other episode, and counts towards its ranking. We also store your sharing preference and when you saw the in-app notice. No new service providers are involved; this data stays with the providers listed in section 6.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in helping listeners discover briefs that other people found worth hearing, while keeping authors anonymous. You have the right to object to this at any time, and you can do so directly in the App as described below.

5.4 Your choice — on by default, off at any time

  • On by default. Sharing is switched on by default. We show you a one-time notice in the App. If you had an account before this feature launched, your briefs become eligible only after a grace period of about 7 days from when you see the notice — unless you switch sharing on yourself earlier.
  • For your whole account: Account → "Share my briefs anonymously in Popular".
  • For a single brief: the sharing switch in that brief's settings.
  • Switching sharing off removes the brief from Popular immediately.

7. Data retention

We keep your account data and generated briefs while your account is active. When you delete a feed, its sources and episodes are removed. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Each daily Popular selection is kept for about 2 days. The anonymous audio copies used for Popular are deleted after at most 4 days. Your sharing preference is kept while your account is active.

8. Your rights & choices

  • Access & deletion — you can delete individual feeds and your entire account from within the App. To request a copy or full deletion of your data, email us. Step-by-step instructions, including deletion without the App installed, are on the Delete your account page.
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Tracking — on iOS you can decline ad tracking at the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • YouTube — you can disconnect your YouTube connection at any time.
  • Popular (anonymous sharing) — you can object to, and stop, anonymous sharing of your briefs at any time: Account → "Share my briefs anonymously in Popular" for all your briefs, or the sharing switch in a brief's settings for a single one. Switching it off removes the brief from Popular immediately.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have additional rights to access, correct, port, or erase your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. Children

Briefcaster is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

11. International transfers

We process and store data on Google Cloud infrastructure located in the European Union (europe-west1). Some service providers may process data in other countries under appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continued use of Briefcaster after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: contact@dataconceptstudio.com.
