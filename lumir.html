Pre-release · iPhone Free to use · Pro unlocks custom presets

A mirror, and a light,
always in your pocket

Lumir turns your iPhone's screen into a mirror and an adjustable light. Four built-in presets get you close to daylight, evening, office or golden-hour light in one tap; a split or full-screen layout puts the mirror and the light exactly where you want them; and when the light is right, one tap saves a photo.

No account, no sign-in, nothing to configure before you start. Everything runs on your phone — see exactly what that means.

See how it works
How it works

Open it, and the light is already right

Four steps, no setup. Everything here runs the moment you open the app.

01 · MIRROR

A mirrored front-camera preview

Lumir shows a live, mirrored preview from the front camera — the same sense of "seeing yourself" as a real mirror, not a recording. Nothing is captured until you ask for it.

02 · LIGHT

The screen becomes the light

Choose full-screen light, or split the screen between mirror and light. Adjust warmth and brightness, or reach for a preset — the light updates instantly.

03 · PRESETS

Four built-in presets

Daylight, Evening, Office and Golden Hour — one tap gets you close to a familiar light, every time. See the presets.

04 · CAPTURE

One tap to keep it

When the light is right, tap the shutter. The photo saves to your library, mirrored to match what you saw — nothing more happens to it than that.

Built-in presets

Four lights, named for how they look

Kelvin describes approximate colour appearance, not a calibrated measurement. Lumir is not a photometric instrument, and the same preset is not guaranteed to look identical on every iPhone or in every room.

Daylight

5500 K

A clear, neutral light close to midday sun.

Evening

3000 K

A warm, low light for late in the day.

Office

4200 K

A brighter, practical light for indoor work.

Golden Hour

3800 K

A warm, flattering light, fast to reach.

Lumir Pro adds custom presets: your own warmth and brightness combinations, saved by name.

Lumir Pro

A one-time unlock, not a subscription

Lumir Pro adds exactly one capability: creating and editing your own custom presets — your own warmth and brightness, saved under a name you choose. Everything else — the mirror, the light, all four built-in presets, split and full layouts, and photo capture — is free and stays free.

Pro is a single purchase. No recurring charge, no renewal, nothing to cancel. If you switch phones, Restore Purchases brings it back through your Apple Account at no extra cost.

Privacy, plainly

Nothing about you leaves your phone

Lumir has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your custom presets and settings are written to a folder on your iPhone that only Lumir and you can reach; they are never sent anywhere. The only outside party involved at all is Apple, when you buy or restore Lumir Pro — Apple takes the payment, and Lumir never sees your payment details.

The full policy, including exactly how each claim was checked against the app's source code, is here: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Both are current drafts, provisional pending final sign-off.

What this is, and what it is not

Plainly

  • — Kelvin values describe approximate colour appearance, not calibrated illumination. Lumir makes no claim of colorimetric accuracy.
  • — Lumir is not a webcam or a video-conferencing tool. It does not integrate with any calling app and cannot supply video to one — using it as a light beside a laptop is a use of the light, not an integration.
  • — Lumir does not detect a folded, hinged or foldable device, and makes no such claim. You choose the layout.
  • — Lumir is not a medical, diagnostic, therapeutic or professional-lighting device. It is a screen showing coloured light.
  • — Lumir requires an iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later. Camera features need camera permission and working camera hardware; Lumir stays fully usable as a light without either.
