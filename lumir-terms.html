2. What Lumir is — and what it does not claim to be

Lumir is a mirror and screen-lighting app for iPhone. It shows a mirrored front-camera preview, turns part or all of the screen into an adjustable light, offers four built-in lighting presets, and lets you take a photo.

Four limits are stated here rather than left for a disappointed review to discover:

  • Kelvin values describe approximate colour appearance, not calibrated illumination. Daylight 5500 K, Evening 3000 K, Office 4200 K and Golden Hour 3800 K are named for how the light looks. Lumir is not a photometric instrument, makes no claim of colorimetric accuracy, and does not promise that the same preset looks identical on two different iPhones or under different ambient light.
  • The split and full layouts are chosen by you. Lumir does not detect a folded, hinged or foldable device and makes no such claim, because no such capability exists in the iOS SDK Lumir is built against. Nothing in the app infers device posture.
  • Lumir is not a webcam or a video-conferencing tool. It does not integrate with any calling app and cannot supply video to one. Using your phone as a light beside a laptop is a use of the light, not an integration.
  • Lumir is not a medical, diagnostic, therapeutic or professional-lighting device. It is a screen showing coloured light. Do not use it as a substitute for anything that matters clinically.

Requirements. iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later. Camera features require camera permission and working camera hardware; Lumir remains fully usable as a light without either.

This is a promise about your own data, and it is worded precisely.

Nothing you made is taken away. If your purchase is refunded, or Apple otherwise revokes it, the custom presets you already created stay on your device and stay usable. You can still:

  • select and apply them, and

What returns to being a Pro capability is authoring: creating a new custom preset, and editing an existing one. Editing is treated the same as creating because changing a preset's warmth or brightness is authoring a different preset in place.
